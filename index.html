WHO WE ARE

A multidisciplinary scientific association

The Scientific Association for Environmental Education and Sustainable Development (EESD) is established to support environmental education, scientific and cultural research, professional cooperation and the advancement of sustainable development.

The Association brings together scientific interests across environmental sciences, biotechnology, biochemistry, environmental health, biodiversity and related fields. Its multidisciplinary structure encourages dialogue between researchers, educators, professionals and institutions.

Through scientific communication, educational initiatives and research collaboration, EESD seeks to strengthen the relationship between scientific knowledge and practical environmental challenges.

SCIENTIFIC RESPONSIBILITY

Research Ethics & Code of Ethics

Ethical research, responsible scientific conduct and the protection of animals and the environment are important activities of the Association.

Issuing a Code of Ethics

The codes of ethics issued by the Ethics Committee of the Scientific Association for Environmental Education and Sustainable Development are included in this list. The archive number of the codes of ethics in this list is registered in the Association's office. Each issued code of ethics is valid for two years.

Based on this list, applicants are issued with a code of ethics certificate. Certificates that do not have the Association's hologram and embossed seal are invalid.

Official Identification of Ethical Codes

The code of ethics is issued by the Ethics Committee with the following specifications, accompanied by an official approval letter bearing the Association's seal and signature, a hologram and an embossed seal.

MRST-CSA-EESD-14004472758-EC-20…

The abbreviations refer to the Ministry of Science, Research and Technology, the Office of the Scientific Associations Commission, the Scientific Association for Environmental Education and Sustainable Development, the national number of the Association and the serial number of its secretariat.

Issued Ethical Codes

This code indicates that the research described in the relevant application has been conducted in accordance with accepted international and national standards, adhering to the principles of ethical treatment of animals and the preservation of habitats and the environment.

Applicants submit their project proposals and supporting documents to the Association's Ethics Committee. The documents and proposals are reviewed by the Committee, and applicants' familiarity with relevant ethical principles and regulations is assessed.
