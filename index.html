WHO WE ARE

A multidisciplinary scientific association

The Scientific Association for Environmental Education and Sustainable Development (EESD) supports environmental education, scientific and cultural research, professional cooperation and the advancement of sustainable development.

The Association brings together scientific interests across environmental sciences, biology, biodiversity, environmental health, education and related disciplines.

OUR MISSION

Advancing Science. Protecting Our Shared Environment.

A KEY SCIENTIFIC SERVICE

Research Ethics & Issuing of Codes of Ethics

The codes of ethics issued by the Ethics Committee of the Scientific Association for Environmental Education and Sustainable Development are registered in the Association's office archive.

Issuing a Code of Ethics

Each issued code of ethics is valid for two years. Based on the registered list, applicants may be issued a code of ethics certificate.

Certificates without the Association's hologram and embossed seal are invalid.

Official certificate requirements

An official approval letter bearing the Association's seal and signature, together with the hologram and embossed seal, is issued to the applicant.

Understanding the Code Number

MRST-CSA-EESD-14004472758-EC-20…

The code format identifies the Ministry of Science, Research and Technology, the Office of the Scientific Associations Commission, the Association, its national number and the serial number registered by the Association's secretariat.

The final serial number must be verified against the Association's official records.

Ethical Review and Research Standards

The code indicates that the research identified in the approved application has been reviewed in relation to applicable national and international standards, including ethical treatment of animals and preservation of habitats and the environment.

Applicants submit their project proposal and supporting documents to the Ethics Committee. Approval and assessment of familiarity with relevant ethical principles and regulations form part of the process.

International Reference Documents

Important international standards and resources for research ethics include:

NIH OACU – Regulations and Standards ↗ PHS Policy on Humane Care and Use of Laboratory Animals (PDF) ↗ Animal Welfare Act and Animal Welfare Regulations ↗ Guide for the Care and Use of Laboratory Animals ↗ AVMA Guidelines for the Euthanasia of Animals: 2020 Edition ↗ Guidance on Prompt Reporting to OLAW ↗ American Society of Animal Science (ASAS) ↗ EESD Animal Protection Bill archive ↗
