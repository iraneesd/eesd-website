WHO WE ARE

A multidisciplinary scientific association

The Scientific Association for Environmental Education and Sustainable Development is a non-governmental, non-profit scientific organization established to promote environmental knowledge, education, research and sustainable development.

The Association encourages scientific exchange, interdisciplinary collaboration, professional development and the dissemination of research findings through educational and research activities.

Its activities are guided by its approved statutes and the objectives of scientific associations.

Our Mission

To advance environmental awareness and sustainable development through scientific education, research cooperation, knowledge exchange and interdisciplinary engagement.

  • Scientific and educational development
  • Environmental research and awareness
  • Interdisciplinary scientific cooperation
  • Knowledge dissemination and professional exchange
  • Support for sustainable development
SCIENTIFIC SCOPE

Research Areas

The Association welcomes interdisciplinary interests and collaboration across the following scientific fields.

🧫

Environmental Microbiology & Microbial Ecology

Microbial diversity, environmental microorganisms, microbial ecology, ecosystem interactions and environmental biotechnology.

🔬

Microbiology & Antimicrobial Resistance

Bacterial diversity, antimicrobial resistance, bacterial pathogens, molecular microbiology and microbial health research.

🧬

Molecular Biology & Biotechnology

Molecular diagnostics, microbial genetics, biotechnology, genomics and applied biological research.

🌱

Environmental Health & One Health

Environmental health, microbial hazards, public health, ecosystem health and the relationship between human, animal and environmental health.

🦋

Biodiversity & Conservation Biology

Taxonomy, biosystematics, zoology, botany, marine biology, biodiversity and conservation.

🌍

Environmental Sciences & Pollution

Environmental engineering, pollution, environmental chemistry, ecology and environmental protection.

🌳

Natural Resources & Ecosystem Management

Forestry, agriculture, rangelands, watershed management, water resources and geology.

📚

Environmental Education & Sustainability

Environmental education, curriculum development, public awareness and sustainable development.
